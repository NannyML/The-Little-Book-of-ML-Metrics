\chapteropener{Sources}{cyanlight}{cyanaccent}{%
The pages of this book are summaries; these are the papers, standards and documentation they were written from, grouped by chapter
and by metric. Where a metric has a founding paper it is listed first. Links were live when the book went to print, and every arXiv
identifier resolves at \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>}.}{}
