We didn't start a book on ML Metrics because it was easy. We started it because we thought it was.

In mid-2024, NannyML was not in a great financial position. We only had a few months of runway left. Every Monday, the whole team would gather and review the status of a few scenarios that could help keep the company alive.

During those weeks, we were all very receptive to new bets and possibilities to extend our runway.

I think it was Hakim (co-founder and CEO of NannyML) who thought that, as one of those bets, we could write a book about ML Metrics, a topic we knew well, sell 20,000 copies, and help extend our runway. He thought we could do this in 3 months.

This right here is delusional thinking. And we all agreed to believe it.

In the end, as many of our first customers know, we didn't finish the book in 3 months. It took us over 2 years. The book didn't save the company. One of the other scenarios ended up happening.

Now with 107 metrics described and 2 years later, we're really happy and at least somewhat surprised to release the book you're holding in your hands right now.

We hope understanding all those metrics is as easy for you as we thought writing this book was gonna be.
